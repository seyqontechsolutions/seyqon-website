Suggested template settings
---------------------------
To email: your business inbox
From name: {{name}}
Reply-to: {{contact}}
Subject: New project enquiry — {{category}}

EmailJS notes
-------------
- The template variables match the enquiry form fields in src/App.jsx.
- The original {{time}} and {{message}} placeholders were mapped to {{contact}} and {{description}} because those are the corresponding form fields.
- The contact field accepts either an email address or phone number. Use {{contact}} as Reply-To only when the visitor enters an email address, or change the form to collect email and phone separately.
- Use a verified address from your connected email service as the From Email; do not use {{contact}} as the sender.
